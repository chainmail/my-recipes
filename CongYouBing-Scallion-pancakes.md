## From: https://redhousespice.com/spring-onion-pancake/
## Makes 8 which is enough for 4 to have with a family meal, and 4 for next time in the freezer!

For the dough
500 g all-purpose flour (plain flour)
320 g hot water
Cooking oil, for coating

For the filling
4 tablespoon melted lard, or coconut oil.
4 tablespoon plain flour.
Chinese style:
  2 teaspoon ground Sichuan pepper, or Chinese five-spice powder
Indian style:
  2 teaspoon garam massala
2 teaspoon salt
160 g finely chopped scallions (green onion/spring onion)

For frying
Chinese style:
  Neutral cooking oil
Indian style:
  Avocado oil
  Indian style requires a hotter oil so olive oil is not appropriate

Make the dough
1. Put flour into a heatproof bowl.
2. Pour in hot water.
3. Stir with chopsticks until no more loose flour or water can be seen.
4. Use your hands to combine the mixture into a rough-looking dough.
5. Tightly cover the dough with cling film and leave to rest for 15 mins.

Prepare the filling
1. While waiting, add melted lard (or other oil), flour, spices and salt to a small bowl.
2. Mix until it becomes a smooth paste.

Shape the pancakes
1. Uncover the dough then knead until it becomes very smooth.
2. Rub a thin layer of oil around the dough, as well as the work surface.
3. With a rolling pin, flatten the dough into a thin, rectangular piece.
4. For your reference about 43cm(17") by 33cm (13").
5. Brush the filling mixture over the dough, then sprinkle finely chopped scallions on top.
6. From the shorter side of the dough piece, roll the dough into a rope (don’t make it too tight).
7. Cut the rope into 4 cylinders.
8. Stand a piece on one end.
9. Press down with your hand, then flatten it with the rolling pin into a pancake that measures around 23cm.

If preparing ahead of plan, this is where you freeze them!

Fry the pancakes
1. In a skillet/frying pan, heat oil over high heat until hot.
2. Drop a piece of scallions in to test.
3. If it sizzles, the oil is hot enough.
4. Turn the heat down to medium and put in the pancakes (the top side facing down).
5. Cover the skillet with a lid and leave to cook for about 2 minutes until the side facing down becomes golden brown.
6. Flip the pancake over, then cover again to cook the other side.
7. Remove them from the pan once the second side turns golden too.
8. For the best result, rest the pancakes on a wire rack for a minute or two before serving (condensation will form if placed on a plate straightaway). Also, the remaining heat will cook the inside further during the resting time.
