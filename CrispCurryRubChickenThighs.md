## From: https://www.ruled.me/crispy-curry-rub-chicken-thighs/

Variations:

1. Chicken with or without bones, always with skin as it needs to crisp up!
2. Prepare the spices as a paste up front. See note 1.

2 tsps curry powder
1 1/2 tsps salt
1 tsp cumin
1 tsp paprika
1 tsp garlic powder
1/2 tsp cayenne pepper
1/2 tsp ground allspice
1/2 tsp chili powder
1/2 tsp ground coriander
1/4 tsp cardamom
1/4 tsp ground cinnamon
1/4 tsp ground ginger
600g bone-in (or not), skin-on chicken thighs
~200ml olive oil

1. Preheat oven to 220C.

2. Gather all the spices and mix together the spices well.

3. Wrap a baking sheet (with edges to contain run off) in foil, then lay the chicken thighs onto the foil. You _really_ want to do this!

4. Rub the olive oil into the chicken, giving each piece of chicken about 5ml Olive Oil. Make sure you do this on both sides. Once oiled, use a spoon to lightly sprinkle the spice mixture over both sides of the chicken and rub it in. Do this a second time with the spices, making sure both sides of the chicken is nicely coated with spice and put the chicken piece on the foil.

5. When complete, wash your hands!

6. Bake the chicken at 220C for about 30-50 minutes. This depends on:
  * Bone in - longer

7. Let cool for 5 minutes or so and serve! Most of the oil and fat will be sitting underneath the chicken, so it’s your choice to either serve this or not. For best flavour and experience, spoon it over the chicken pieces before serving to plate.

8. Garnish with red pepper flakes and chives.

Keep the bones and remaining juices to make chicken stock for biryani, etc.

Notes:

1. Melt about 1-2 tbsp of butter (i.e. flora block), add 1 tsp minced ginger and lightly stir to distribute the ginger. Then add the spices about 1/3 in 3 steps. Stirring to mix the spices as you add them. Once they are cooked together (30-60 seconds) put the spices in a bowl and leave to cool. They should be very aromtaic at this point.
