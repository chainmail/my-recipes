## From: https://mymorningmocha.com/ginger-biscuit-recipe/#recipe
## Is vegetarian and can be vegan (margarine)

230 g Self-Raising Flour
3 tbsp Ground Ginger (Buy from Indian supermarkets, it's cheap!)
2 tsp cinnamon
130 g Light Brown Sugar (La Perruche)
2 tbsp Golden Syrup (or make your own: https://mymorningmocha.com/golden-syrup-substitutes/)
125 g Margarine Or Butter (e.g. 1 margarine stick)
1 tsp Bicarbonate Of Soda (Do not over do this!)

Instructions
 
1. Preheat your oven to 185°C (gas mark 4, 160°Fan) and line two baking trays with parchment paper.
2. Melt butter/margarine in a bowl in the microwave.
3. Add light brown sugar, ginger, cinnamon, baking powder and golden syrup to a large mixing bowl and stir to combine well.
4. Once melted, pour it to the mixing bowl.
5. Add flour to the mixing bowl and mix together until a dough has formed.
  - Add the spices first then baking powder then flour; mixing at each stage.
4. Put the big dough ball in the fridge for 30 minutes to firm up.
5. Scoop a tablespoon of dough and roll it into a ball using the palm of your hands.
  - Place the ball onto a lined baking tray.
  - Repeat this step for the remaining dough; leave a gap between each ball.
  - Gently press the dough with your palm to flatten slightly.
  - They will not spread very much in the oven, so flatten them to achieve a good biscuit through out the dough.
6. Bake in the oven for 10 to 12 minutes or until golden brown. Once baked, leave the biscuits to cool down for at least 10 minutes.
