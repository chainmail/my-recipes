# From: https://www.wellplated.com/chicken-thigh-marinade/#wprm-recipe-container-91367

Made slightly more inflammatory.

2 garlic cloves, roughly chopped (about 2 tablespoons)
1 tablespoon extra virgin olive oil
0.25 tablespoons low-sodium soy sauce
0.25 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
1 large teaspoon Dijon mustard
2 teaspoons ground cumin
1 teaspoon ground turmeric
¼ teaspoon kosher salt
Juice of 1/2 lemon
2 skinless chicken thighs (on the bone)

Mix it all up.
Make cuts in the chicken thigh flesh.
Put everything together, rubbing the marinade into the chicken cuts, in a glass bowl for >2 hours.
Cover with cling film.

When ready, heat the oven to 190C and bake for 25 minutes.

Serve with zoodles or rice.
