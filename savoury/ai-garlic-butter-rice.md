# Garlic butter rice

* Garlic butter rice is a flavorful side dish made by sautéing garlic in butter, toasting long-grain white rice in the mixture, and simmering it in chicken broth until tender

## Method

* Sauté minced garlic in butter or butter alternative until fragrant
  * Do not burn the garlic to avoid bitterness
* Toast the rice for 1–2 minutes to coat grains and enhance nutty flavor
* Add some chicken stock to steam the rice through:
  * If you are using pre-cooked rice, this is to ensure it gets steamed throughly so add a small amount of hot stock
  * If you are using uncooked rice, this is where you cook it, so add enough hot chicken stock
* Resting the rice off-heat for 5–10 minutes before fluffing with a fork
  *  Add remaining butter or fresh herbs like parsley and chives

Serve with things like cordon bleu pork or chicken schnitzel, with a side of salad
