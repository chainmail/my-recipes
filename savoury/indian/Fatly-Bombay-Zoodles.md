## Bombay Zoodles
This was my idea, and assisted by AI for speed/laziness

Prep the masala in advance if short on time. The zoodles need to be prepped, and used then served.

### Zoodle prep (do this first, ~20 min)

1. Spiralize 4 medium zucchini (~900 g) into 3–4 mm noodles.
2. Toss with **1 tsp fine salt** in a large bowl. The salt draws out the water.
3. Let sit **15–20 min**. You'll see a pool of liquid form.
4. Drain off the liquid.
5. Gather the zoodles into a ball, wrap tightly in **kitchen paper**, and squeeze with both hands for 10–15 seconds. Unwrap, discard the paper.
6. Repeat with fresh paper — squeeze again. You should be able to lift a handful with barely a drip.
7. Pat any remaining moisture on the work surface. Set the zoodles aside.

The two-squeeze step is what makes the difference between a coated dish and a watery one. One squeeze leaves too much free water that dilutes the masala on contact.

### Masala

1. Heat **3 tbsp oil** in a wide, heavy pan over medium-high heat.
2. Temper **½ tsp mustard seeds** + **1 tsp cumin seeds** until they pop.
3. Add **1 onion** (sliced), **½ dsp ginger paste**, **½ dsp garlic paste**, **1 green chili** (slit, seeds in for spice and flavour or deseeded for just flavour), pinch of salt. Cook 5–6 min until the onions are golden.
4. Add **¼ tsp turmeric**, **1 tsp ground coriander**, **1 tsp ground cumin**, **½–1 tsp chili powder**, **2 tbsp tomato paste**. Stir 1 min.
5. Add **4 tomatoes** (chopped & deseeded) or a **400 g can**. Cook, mashing, until the mixture thickens and oil pools at the edges — ~8 min. Set the tomato hearts to one side.
6. Stir in **1 tsp garam masala** and **1 tsp lemon juice**. Off heat.

### Combine

1. Back on medium-high heat. Add the tomato hearts from the deseeded tomatoes and stir, cook in for 2-3 minutes.
2. Toss the zoodles in and **toss hard for 60–90 seconds** — just until hot and coated.
3. Off heat. Scatter with **fresh coriander** and a pinch of **amchoor** (dried mango powder).

Serve immediately — they'll keep softening on the plate.
