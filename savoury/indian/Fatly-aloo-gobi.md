# From: https://www.indianhealthyrecipes.com/bombay-potatoes

Follow the recipe except:
* Do not add garam masala to the water boiling potatoes. It brings almost no extra flavour and garam masala can be expensive.
* Do add the turmeric to the water. It is used as a visual appetiser to colour the potatoes just as you find them in restaurants.
* You can also add cauliflower
  * Don't boil it, just let it cook together in the mix so it will retain some texture
