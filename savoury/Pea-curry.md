From: https://www.thefieryvegetarian.com/pea-curry/

Ingredients

    3 small onions (roughly chopped)
    2 large green chilli peppers (roughly chopped)
    3 tsp ginger paste
    3 tsp garlic paste
    1 tbsp ground coriander
    1 teaspoon cumin
    1 teaspoon turmeric
    1 can tomatoes
    1 tetrapak coconut milk 250ml
    ½ teaspoon sugar
    1-2 teaspoon garam masala
    600g frozen peas
    Squeeze of lemon juice to serve, optional

Instructions

    Sauté the onion, garlic, ginger, and chilli pepper until aromatic.
    Add ground coriander, turmeric, and cumin.
        - Can add a splash of vinegar to bring the powdered spices together with the other ingredients.
    Add tomatoes and stir. Put to one side and cool.
    Blend until smooth.
    Return to the saucepan and add the coconut milk and heat on high until boiling.
    Reduce the heat to medium-high to maintain a lively strong for 10 minutes.
    Add salt to taste.
    Add frozen peas, sugar, and garam masala and turn the heat up high again until the mixture is bubbling.
    Reduce to medium-high and cook for a further ten minutes.
    Add an optional squeeze of lemon juice to serve.
